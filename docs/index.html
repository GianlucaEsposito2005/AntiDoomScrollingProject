Statement

Hello! If you are here, you are probably like me and spend way too much time each day doom scrolling. You might not even get any enjoyment out of it, yet you do it out of habit anyway. Like me, you may have even tried deleting apps like Instagram but find yourself just redownloading them anyway because you message people on there. You may also just give out your Instagram to new people you meet instead of your phone number, which makes not having it inconvenient. You could try just texting on your laptop, but that gets annoying fast. You may have also tried turning your phone black and white, but let's be honest, that does not last long. The second you need to see something in colour for more than a few seconds, congrats, you are back to colour on your phone. You may have even downloaded apps like SocialLite that promise to get rid of intrusive features like Instagram Reels but slowly start scrolling your home page anyway because removing everything is a feature stuck behind paywalls that you do not want to pay for. The situation may seem hopeless, but fear not, for it is not (I think).

The solution? To replace Instagram and other doom-scrolling apps with other media. There are things I want to learn and books and comics I want to read, yet I never find the time for them. By removing doom-scrolling time while still allowing myself downtime to not be productive, I can do just that. Hopefully, I can get around to doing things that I have been wanting to do for a while. To keep me accountable, I have this page, which I will need to update every day with something I learned that did not come from my courses and some other piece of media I consumed that day that was not doom-scrolling-related.

Why Doom Scrolling is Bad

Doom scrolling is not just a waste of time; it is also slowly rotting our brains away. There is a reason it is called brain rot or slop. The content is low quality and meant to keep you addicted. You are not critically thinking, learning, or even engaging. Nevermind the ragebait, social media can make you mad or anxious for longer periods than you need to be. That is not to say do not stay informed but everything in moderation or else you will drive yourself crazy. Aditionally, after spending alot of time with short form content it can feel hard to do other more mundane tasks such as watching a movie, studying, working or reading.
